🚀 Excited to share my latest project as a Data Analytics Intern at **Alfido Tech**!

I recently completed **Task 1: Customer Behavior Analysis**, where I analyzed **250,000 transaction records** across **49,673 unique customers** (2020–2023) to understand customer lifetime value, purchasing habits, and churn dynamics.

Here is a breakdown of what I worked on and the key insights uncovered:

📊 **Methodology & Workflow:**
1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Handled missing return records, parsed transaction timestamps, and normalized customer demographic fields in Python using Pandas.
2. **Feature Engineering:** Aggregated transaction logs into customer-level RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) metrics, tracking return rates and preferred product categories.
3. **RFM Segmentation:** Segmented the customer base into 5 distinct behavioral cohorts using quintile scoring (`qcut`).
4. **Retention & Churn Analysis:** Evaluated month-over-month engagement trends and inactivity risks.

💡 **Key Findings:**
• **High Revenue Concentration:** Champions (17.6%) and Loyal Customers (24.9%) make up 42.5% of the customer base but drive **55.8% ($380.1M)** of total company revenue.
• **Critical At-Risk Opportunity:** 8,711 previously active customers (17.5% of base) have lapsed for an average of **411.5 days**, putting **$140.7M** in historical revenue at risk.
• **Core Category Drivers:** Books ($204.9M) and Clothing ($204.5M) generate 60.0% of gross sales.
• **Operational Bottleneck:** Recorded product returns averaged **49.8%**, highlighting a crucial need to improve sizing guides and product specifications.
• **Overall Churn Rate:** Customer account churn stands at **20.0%**, with steady monthly active customer retention (~5,400 active monthly buyers).

🎯 **5 Actionable Recommendations for Alfido Tech:**
1. **Targeted Win-Back Campaign:** Launch personalized email discount sequences (15% return incentive) to reactivate At-Risk customers, recovering an estimated $14M–$21M.
2. **VIP Champions Program:** Introduce early product drops, priority support, and free expedited shipping for top spenders ($21.8k avg spend).
3. **Nurture Recent Buyers:** Implement automated post-purchase recommendations and bundle discounts to migrate recent buyers into high-frequency loyal customers.
4. **Category Bundling & Return Mitigation:** Cross-sell accessories with Books and Clothing while overhauling size charts to cut return costs.
5. **Automated CRM Inactivity Alerts:** Trigger re-engagement notifications when customer inactivity passes 180 days to curb the 20% churn baseline.
